1.Introduction

Livora (“the App”) is the official guest app for Corbett The Vedant By Livora (“we”, “us”, “our”), a resort in the Jim Corbett National Park belt, Uttarakhand, India. Through the App, guests can explore the resort, check room availability, make bookings, manage their stay, message the resort team, request room service, manage their profile, and reach us by call, WhatsApp or directions.

This Privacy Policy explains what personal information we collect through the App and our booking backend, why we collect it, who may handle it, and how you can control it. By using the App, you acknowledge this policy. If you do not agree with it, please do not use the App.

2.Information We Collect

We collect the following information, mostly directly from you when you register, book, or get in touch.

Name
To identify you on your account and bookings.
Email address
To create your account, contact you about your booking, and respond to enquiries.
Phone number
To reach you about your booking or stay.
Account and login information
Details needed to sign you in and keep your account secure. We do not display or share your password with resort staff.
Booking and stay details
Room selected, dates, number of guests, booking status, and other details of your reservation and stay.
Guest messages and service requests
Messages you send to the resort team and room-service requests you place, including their content and timing.
Information you choose to provide
Anything else you enter in the App, such as profile details, preferences or special requests.
Basic technical and device information
Only where necessary to run and protect the App, such as app version, device type, operating system version and error or security logs.

What we do not collect. The current version of the App does not collect payment card or bank details, does not track your location in the background, and does not access your contacts or microphone. It does not include advertising SDKs. When you tap call, WhatsApp or directions, the App simply opens your phone dialer, WhatsApp or a maps app.

3.How We Use Information

We use your information to:

  • create and manage your account;
  • process and manage room bookings;
  • provide services during your stay;
  • handle room-service requests;
  • respond to your messages and enquiries;
  • provide customer support;
  • send important service-related communications, such as booking confirmations, changes or account notices; and
  • improve the security and reliability of the App.

The current version of the App does not send push notifications while it is closed. We do not use your information for advertising.

4.How Information Is Shared

We do not sell your personal information. We share it only in these limited ways:

  • Resort team. Staff at Corbett The Vedant By Livora see the information they need to manage your booking, stay, messages and requests.
  • Service providers. Your information may be processed by the providers that host and operate the App and its backend. They handle it on our behalf to keep the App running.
  • Legal and safety reasons. We may disclose information where required by law or legal process, or where reasonably necessary to protect our rights, our guests and our property, or to prevent fraud or misuse.

When you choose to call, message on WhatsApp or get directions, the information you share there is handled by those apps under their own policies. The App does not use the WhatsApp API; it only opens WhatsApp so you can contact the resort.

5.Data Storage and Security

Your information is stored on servers operated by our infrastructure providers, which may be located in India or in other countries. We use reasonable technical and organizational safeguards to protect it, such as limiting access to authorized personnel and using secure connections where supported.

No method of storage or transmission over the internet is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security. Please keep your login details confidential and contact us promptly if you suspect unauthorized use of your account.

6.Data Retention

We keep your information only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes described in this policy, including to provide the App and your stay, meet legal and accounting requirements, resolve disputes, prevent fraud, and support our legitimate business needs. When information is no longer needed, we delete it or de-identify it.

7.Account Deletion

You can ask us to delete your Livora account and the personal information linked to it at any time. To do so:

  1. Email Livorahospitality06@gmail.com with the subject “Account Deletion Request – Livora”.
  2. Send it from the email address registered on your account, and include your name and registered phone number so we can verify your identity.
  3. We will confirm by email once your request has been processed, within a reasonable time.

Draft the deletion email

Deleting your account removes your profile and the personal information linked to it from the App. If you have an upcoming or active booking, we may need to complete or cancel it first.

Some information may need to be kept where the law requires it or where it is necessary for legitimate records, such as booking and accounting records, dispute resolution or fraud prevention. We keep only what is needed and for no longer than necessary.

8.User Rights and Choices

You can contact us at any time to:

  • Access the personal information we hold about you;
  • Correct information that is inaccurate or out of date;
  • Request deletion of your account and personal information (see Account Deletion above); and
  • Ask questions about how your information is handled.

You decide what optional information to share with us, though some details are needed to make and manage a booking. You can also manage app permissions in your device settings, or stop using the App by uninstalling it. Depending on where you live, you may have additional rights under applicable law. We may need to verify your identity before acting on a request.

9.Children’s Privacy

The App is not specifically directed toward children. Children should not provide personal information through the App without appropriate authorization from a parent or legal guardian. If you believe a child’s personal information has been provided to us without proper authorization, please contact us and we will take reasonable steps to delete it.

10.Third-Party Services

The App and its backend rely on third-party infrastructure and service providers that are necessary to operate them, such as cloud hosting, database and storage services. Our website, livorhospitality.vercel.app, is hosted on Vercel. The App is distributed through Google Play and runs on Android, and features such as calling, WhatsApp and directions open other apps on your device.

We use these services only to run the App and serve our guests, not for advertising. Each provider’s own privacy policy applies to how it handles information under its control.

11.Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this policy from time to time, for example when the App changes or when the law requires. The “Last updated” date at the top of this page shows when it was last revised. If we make significant changes, we will take reasonable steps to let you know, such as an update in the App or an email. Continued use of the App after an update means you accept the revised policy.

12.Contact Us

For questions about this policy, to access or correct your information, or to request account deletion, please get in touch.
